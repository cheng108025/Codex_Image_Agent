# Image_To_Images_AI_Agent｜20 種圖片轉圖片提示詞

## 使用方式

每張圖的實際提示詞依下列順序組成：

1. `共同角色與構圖鎖定提示詞`
2. 對應編號的 `風格提示詞`
3. `共同結尾約束`

輸入圖片：`01-gentle-snow-blue.png`（嚴格編輯目標，不是單純靈感參考）。

## 共同角色與構圖鎖定提示詞

```text
Use case: identity-preserve style-transfer.
Input image: Image 1 is the strict edit target.
Change ONLY the rendering medium and visual style. Preserve as exactly as possible the original single character's facial identity, gentle smile, blue eye shape and gaze, blush, white wavy hairstyle and every lock of hair, white cat ears with pink interiors, black hair bow, snowflake ornament, gold and blue hanging jewels, neck bow and pendant, white-and-ice-blue off-shoulder dress, all garment edges and decorations, pose, anatomy, centered front-facing bust framing, crop, scale, object placement, pale-blue circular snowy backdrop and white outer corners. Keep the same number, position, size and orientation of every visible element. Do not redesign the character, costume, accessories or background. Do not add, remove, move or replace anything.
```

## 共同結尾約束

```text
Apply the style consistently to both character and existing background while keeping their exact layout. No new motifs, props, scenery, text, logo, signature or watermark.
```
